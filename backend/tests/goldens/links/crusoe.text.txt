Frequently asked  questions

How is the interview process structured? How long does it take?

The process will vary based on the team and role you’re applying for. Typically, it includes a recruiter screen, one or more technical or skills-based interviews, and a final round where you'll meet members of the team. Most processes wrap up within two to six weeks. We aim to make the experience transparent — we want you to walk away knowing whether Crusoe is the right fit for you.

Is there room to grow my career at Crusoe?

Yes. Crusoe is scaling rapidly across multiple disciplines, which creates real opportunity for people who want to take on more responsibility or develop expertise in emerging areas. We're still building many of our teams and processes — which means you can influence how things get done.

What does Crusoe expect from candidates’ AI fluency?

We build AI infrastructure for the world; we expect you to use AI tools. Use them to prepare, to sharpen your thinking, and to communicate more clearly. What we're evaluating is your judgment, your experience, and how you think — not whether you drafted your cover letter solo. During live interviews and assessments, we want to hear your voice, not a generated script. AI as a collaborator: yes.
