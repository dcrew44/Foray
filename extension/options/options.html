Foray

Fills application forms with the answers Foray prepared, in your own browser.

Connect to Foray

Foray runs on your own machine or server. The extension talks to it and to nothing else.

The address you open Foray at, for example http://localhost:3000.

In Foray, open Settings, find Browser extension and make a token, then paste it here. Foray shows it once, and this page never shows it again.

What it does

  • On a job application page it asks your Foray whether an application is prepared for that page. If one is, it shows a small panel.
  • When you press Fill the form, it puts the prepared answers into the form and attaches your resume. It marks what it filled and what it left, and stops.
  • You read the form and press submit.
  • When the employer's page confirms the application went in, it tells Foray, with the page's words and, if you started from the toolbar button, a picture of the page.

What it never does

  • It never presses submit, unless you turned that on in Foray's Settings, and then only for an application answered entirely from what you saved. Even then it stops when the page shows a CAPTCHA, a sign-in or an account to create.
  • It never solves or works around a CAPTCHA.
  • It never signs in and never creates an account.
  • It never makes up an answer, and never ticks an agreement box: terms, privacy, marketing.
  • It never disguises itself: no fake typing, no hiding that a script filled a field.
  • It sends nothing anywhere except to your own Foray.

What it can reach

  • The job boards Foray reads (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Gem, Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS and Workday), to show its panel there.
  • Any other page only when you click Foray in the toolbar on it, and only that tab.
  • Your Foray address, which Chrome asks you to allow when you press Save.
  • The address and token above, kept in this browser profile and nowhere else.
